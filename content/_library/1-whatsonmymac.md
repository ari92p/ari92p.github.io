# What's on my mac

This is what I installed and dont want to miss.

**General**

- [Brave](https://brave.com/) is my main browser
- [Claude](https://claude.ai/) and ChatGPT (Codex) as helpers when I write code or text

**Building things**

- [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) and [Xcode](https://developer.apple.com/xcode/) for code
- [Docker](https://www.docker.com/) for trying out containers
- [Homebrew](https://brew.sh/) installs most of the rest
- [Parallels Desktop](https://www.parallels.com/products/desktop/) and [CrossOver](https://www.codeweavers.com/products/crossover-mac/) when something needs Windows

**Network and safety**

- [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) connects the Mac to my homelab from anywhere
- [Mullvad VPN](https://mullvad.net/) for everything else
- [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/) as the password manager, talking to my own server

**Video and 3D**

- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) with the Blackmagic RAW tools for editing
- [DJI Studio](https://www.dji.com/) imports and previews the footage from my DJI camera
- [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) for 3D
- [IINA](https://iina.io/) plays everything else

**Small helpers for the Mac itself**

- [Maccy](https://maccy.app/) keeps a history of the clipboard
- [LinearMouse](https://linearmouse.org/) sets how my mouse moves and scrolls
- [Scroll Reverser](https://pilotmoon.com/scrollreverser/) gives the mouse and the trackpad their own scroll direction
- [SaneSideButtons](https://janhuelsmann.com/sanesidebuttons) makes the side buttons of my mouse go back and forward in every app
- [MiddleClick](https://github.com/artginzburg/MiddleClick) turns a tap with three fingers on the trackpad into a middle click
- [Logi Options+](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/software/logi-options-plus.html) configures my Logitech mouse
- [TG Pro](https://www.tunabellysoftware.com/tgpro/) shows temperatures and controls the fans
- [DaisyDisk](https://daisydiskapp.com/) shows what fills the disk
- [AppCleaner](https://freemacsoft.net/appcleaner/) removes apps for good
- [Keka](https://www.keka.io/) opens and creates archives, balenaEtcher writes disk images
- [DisplayVolumeKeys](/projects/display-volume-keys/) is my own app and lets the volume keys control my monitor

**Games and streaming**

- [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/about/) and [Battle.net](https://www.battle.net/)
- [Moonlight](https://moonlight-stream.org/) to stream games from my gaming PC to the Mac
