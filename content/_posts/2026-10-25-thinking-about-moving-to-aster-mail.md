title: Thinking about moving from Tuta to Aster Mail
date: 2026-10-25 08:00:00
projects: [personal]
---
When I started to de-google my life, email was the first thing I wanted out of Gmail. It is the account everything else hangs on, and it holds years of my history. So I moved to [Tuta Mail](https://tuta.com), and it has served me well.

Tuta encrypts the mailbox on your device, the company is based in Germany, and the apps are open source. For a Gmail replacement that is what I wanted. The price of that design is that Tuta does not speak normal IMAP, so I am tied to its own apps and the web client. What I also don't like is that I can't get rid of aliases once I have created them, and there is a maximum number you can create.

Lately I have been looking at [Aster Mail](https://astermail.org). It is a newer encrypted mail service with the same basic idea: encryption happens on your device, and the service only sees the routing data it needs to deliver a message. It also advertises post-quantum encryption on top of PGP. It has an active Discord server where you can talk to the developers directly.

The part that also got my attention is the free plan. With Aster I can use an address on my own domain for free. With Tuta that only works in a paid plan. For someone who already owns a few domains and runs a homelab, an address like `me@my-own-domain` is a real argument, and it keeps me from being tied to a provider's domain forever.

I have not moved yet. Tuta stays until Aster has proven itself. If I move, I will write down how it went.
