This is the new home of ari92p. I wanted a place that is mine, without an algorithm, ads or sign-up, so I built one the way the web looked in 1996: a black bar on the left, a white column in the middle and buttons that look like they came from a CD-ROM.

Here is what you find:

- **News** is this page. It is also my blog: short notes about things I build and the mistakes along the way.
- **Projects** shows what I made, one page per project.
- **Library** holds tools and notes I like to keep at hand.
- **Inventory** lists the gear I own and use.
- **Market** has things I sell.

The links to GitHub, Discord, Pixiv and Twitter are in the black bar.
