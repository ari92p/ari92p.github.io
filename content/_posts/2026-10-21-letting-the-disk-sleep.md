title: Letting the disk sleep
date: 2026-10-21 11:00:00

The big hard drive in my NAS is meant to sleep when nobody needs it. The sleep setting was on, but I wanted proof that it works.

The proof is a counter inside the drive that goes up every time it spins up. At the first reading it stood at 799 after about 3,100 hours of use, which is roughly six wake-ups per day. Too many for a drive that should be asleep half of the day.

So I gathered all background work into one block in the morning: the backups, the photo library scan, the update runs and the media tasks. The night and the evening stay quiet, and the drive can sleep through them. Now I read the counter again after a few days and see whether it moved less.

**Lesson:** a setting that is on does not mean it works. Find a number that proves it.
