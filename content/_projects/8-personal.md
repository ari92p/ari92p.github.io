<p class="it">This is not a program. It is the long running project of getting my own things out of the hands of big companies, and writing down what works and what does not.</p>
<p>It started with email, because the mailbox is the account everything else hangs on. I left Gmail for <a href="https://tuta.com">Tuta Mail</a>, which encrypts the mailbox on the device, and I am now thinking about moving on to <a href="https://astermail.org">Aster Mail</a>, mainly because its free plan allows an address on my own domain.</p>
<p>The same idea runs through the rest of my setup: a password manager that talks to my own server, and a DNS filter with its own resolver, both running in my <a href="/projects/homelab/">homelab</a>. Whenever I change something, I write a short post about it.</p>
</div>
