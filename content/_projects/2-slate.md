<div>
<p class="it">Browsers let you keep your whole history or wipe all of it. Slate sits in between: it removes only the sites you pick, and leaves the rest alone.</p>
<p><b>Slate</b> cleans with your own block list and allow list and with filter lists you subscribe to. The allow list always wins. One click on the toolbar icon removes the matching history entries. Two public curated lists are included as a ready-made start, and each one can be switched off, removed and restored.</p>
<p>New matching visits are noticed as they happen, even pages that redirect somewhere else. Slate also removes the address bar suggestions the browser learned for the start pages of cleaned sites.</p>
</div>
<div>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>The icon</legend>Chalk writing on the slate means there are matches. A tick means everything is clean.</fieldset>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Lists</legend>Subscribe to any HTTPS filter list, the same kind DNS and content blockers use.</fieldset>
</div>

<div class="boxrow">
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Schedule</legend>Checks at browser start or hourly, every 6 or 12 hours, or daily.</fieldset>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Cookies</legend>Optional. The permission is asked for only when you turn it on, and it may sign you out of matching sites.</fieldset>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Good to know</legend>Deletion cannot be undone. Visits synced from other devices stay hidden from the extension.</fieldset>
</div>
