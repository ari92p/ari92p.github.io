<p class="it">Crunchyroll has no button for Picture-in-Picture. This extension adds one, right inside the player controls, so the video can float in a small window while you do something else.</p>
<p><b>Crunchyroll PiP:</b> the button sits in the right-hand controls, directly to the left of the subtitle button. It appears only on episode pages and only after playback has started, so it never gets in the way elsewhere.</p>
</div>

<figure class="hero"><img src="/static/img/projects/crunchyroll-pip/preview.png" width="640" alt="The Crunchyroll player with the new Picture-in-Picture button next to the subtitle button" height="281"><figcaption>The new button, left of the subtitle button, with its tooltip.</figcaption></figure>

<div class="boxrow stepsrow">
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Open</legend>Open a Crunchyroll episode.</fieldset>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Play</legend>Start the video. The button appears once playback has started.</fieldset>
<fieldset class="tbox"><legend>Float</legend>Click the PiP button in the player controls, or use the keyboard shortcut.</fieldset>
</div>
