Two cameras for two jobs: an action camera for rough conditions and a pocket gimbal camera for everything else.

**[DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro (Adventure Combo)](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B07FW4CZZL).** A waterproof 4K action camera with a 1/1.3-inch sensor and two OLED touchscreens. The Adventure Combo comes with three Extreme Battery Plus batteries and more accessories.

- [DJI Osmo Action ND filter set](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B07FQSPPWR), fits the Osmo Action 3, 4 and 5 Pro
- [DJI street photography grip kit](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0G5PNHVLZ) for the Osmo Action 5 Pro
- [Ulanzi VL04 selfie stick](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0FR9F875Z), a monopod that extends to 114 cm, with a shoulder strap
- [DJI Osmo neck mount, large](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0F8W21VHV)
- [SmallRig magnetic quick-release neck mount (5360)](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0FHDBMQD3) for action cameras
- [SmallRig suction cup mount](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0C6LRCFLV) with a 360° ball head, for the car
- [SmallRig mini camera bag](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B09Q5R1LJ6), 16 × 12 cm

**[DJI Osmo Pocket 4 (Creator Combo)](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0FKT9K6CB).** A pocket-sized camera with a 1-inch CMOS sensor, a 3-axis gimbal and 4K video up to 240 frames per second for slow motion. The Creator Combo comes with a DJI Mic 3 transmitter, a fill light, a wide-angle lens and a mini tripod.

- [K&F Concept VND filter set](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0FR8X43Y9), fits the Osmo Pocket 4 and 3
- [Ulanzi PK16 magnetic holder stand](https://www.amazon.de/dp/B0FYCRKKF6), a foldable stand with a quick-release clamp that tilts 270°
